01

IoU and Pointing answer different localization questions.

Intersection-over-Union
area(prediction ∩ target) / area(prediction ∪ target)

Rewards regional overlap. Sensitive to threshold, smoothing bandwidth, target size, and map spread.

Pointing game
1 if argmax(prediction) lies inside target, else 0

Asks whether the most confident point hits the ellipse. Ignores the quality of the rest of the map.

A change can improve IoU without changing Pointing: the map may overlap the ellipse more accurately while its highest point remains on the same side of the boundary. This matters for the spatial-language control.

03

The current evidence supports three claims at different strengths.

Claim 1 · strong

Position-aware, language-conditioned attention improves over temporal alignment alone.

Final exceeds Temporal-only on both metrics and across all five training seeds. In the primary seed, the paired median IoU difference is +0.0289 and the Pointing difference is +0.0421.

Claim 2 · strong

The improvement depends on true fixation-to-position correspondence.

Permuting positions within each instance causes a large collapse in both metrics across all five seeds. The coordinate set remains, but its relationship to each fixation’s time and language context is broken.

Claim 3 · qualified

Spatial words make a smaller contribution, especially to region overlap.

Masking spatial descriptors reduces IoU significantly in all five seeds. Pointing decreases directionally in all five, but reaches significance in only two. The language claim is therefore weaker and metric-dependent.

04

Ablation removes a component. A control tests an explanation.

The two PR #3 controls are useful because they preserve most of the trained model while selectively damaging information at evaluation time.

Position shuffle

Alternative explanation

“The model only needs the overall gaze distribution or extra capacity.”

Manipulation

Permute x/y among fixations inside the same instance.

Expected evidence

A large collapse means the real feature-to-coordinate pairing matters.

Spatial-word mask

Alternative explanation

“Report features only repeat the finding label.”

Manipulation

Zero left/right/bilateral/upper/lower/middle/retrocardiac.

Expected evidence

A decrease isolates location-bearing language from label redundancy.

One remaining alternative

Position shuffling does not replace a label-conditioned anatomy baseline. The final model receives a finding embedding and may learn where that finding usually occurs. A direct anatomy-prior comparison is still required.

05

RadZero is a competitive reference, not a modality verdict.

RadZero localizes a phrase from image patches and text. GazeMed Final localizes from gaze and speech without image pixels. On the matched instances, Final has higher IoU and statistically tied Pointing.

06

Keep the evidence boundary visible.

Observed
  • Results on 1,093 instances from one patient split
  • Five training-seed checks with a fixed split
  • Position-shuffle and language-mask controls
  • Matched RadZero evaluation
Supported
  • Learned timing is better than a fixed lag
  • Real fixation coordinates are used
  • Spatial words contribute to IoU
  • Behavior-only localization is competitive here
Open
  • Causal, real-time performance
  • New split and new-reader stability
  • Value beyond anatomy priors
  • Clinical mention-link accuracy
  • Cross-institution and device transfer
Preliminary result discipline

Five initialization seeds measure optimization variability. They do not measure cohort variability because the patient partition is unchanged. Additional grouped splits are needed before a robust generalization claim.
